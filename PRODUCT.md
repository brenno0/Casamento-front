# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Wedding guests use the public site and their private invitation to learn event details, RSVP, browse gifts, and share messages. The couple uses a private management panel to organize wedding planning.

## Product Purpose

The product brings the couple's wedding information, guest invitation flow, and planning tools together. Success means guests can find trustworthy details and respond, while the couple can manage the event in one place.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site has a public wedding page, token-based private invitations, RSVP, ceremony and party details, maps, dress guidance, story, gallery, gifts, guest message wall, and authenticated planning panel. Preserve existing names, dates, event facts, content, and working functions. Public content is in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Evidence on Hand

The site identifies Nayane and Brenno, with wedding date 17 July 2027. Supplied couple photographs and a watercolor church illustration are available in the project assets. Do not invent missing event facts or guest testimonials.

## Product Principles

- Keep guest information clear and easy to find.
- Preserve invitation privacy and RSVP behavior.
- Keep planning tools usable for the couple.
- Use real couple and venue imagery when it helps guests connect with the event.
