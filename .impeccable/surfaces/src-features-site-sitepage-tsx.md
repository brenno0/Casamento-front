# Guest site (home and invite)

Scope: `/` and `/c/:token`, visitor mode Persuade. Audience: wedding guests, mostly on phones from a WhatsApp link. Job: learn when and where, confirm presence, give a gift, leave a message. The couple's panel inherits the same tokens in Operate register.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Mesa Posta, the set wedding table. Every guest finds their own place card first; the site is the table laid for them. Refuses the cream page with script names and floral corners.

OWN-WORLD: cool white linen ground, bottle-green ink that owns whole full-bleed bands (the runner), butter candle-yellow for selected states, white paper cards with sharp corners and a fine inner rule, engraved Caslon display type over a plain humanist sans, photographs as prints laid on the cloth.

STORY: the guest sees whose wedding and when, finds their name, reads where to go, confirms presence, then browses story, gifts and messages.

FIRST VIEWPORT: linen field; names stacked at display scale on the left, date and running countdown set beneath like a menu card; a large photo print on the right when the gallery has one. On an invite link the guest's place card with their name and personal message sits directly under the date.

FORM: the set table, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key d6b27f03. Signature interaction: the folded place card that opens on the invite link. Raises: story as strict dated photo sequence; one colour committed in full bands; scale by importance.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
